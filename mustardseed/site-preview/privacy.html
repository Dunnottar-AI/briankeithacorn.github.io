Privacy Policy

Draft. Last updated 2026.

This page explains, in plain words, what Mustard Seed does with your information. The short answer: very little.

No accounts, no ads

You do not need an account to use the app. There are no ads. We do not sell your data, and we do not use trackers or analytics.

What stays on your phone

Some things, like your settings or saved verses, are stored on your own device (in your browser's local storage). We do not see them. You can remove them by clearing your browser data or removing the app.

Hosting logs

The company that hosts the app may keep basic logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of a visit. This is normal for websites and helps keep them working and safe.

When you send us a request

If you ask for Mustard Seed for your church, we get the details you send by email. We use them only to reply to you about getting the app. We do not share them or add you to a mailing list.

Children and families

Mustard Seed is meant to be safe for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The kids' features in the app collect nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Questions? Email hello@getmustardseed.org.
