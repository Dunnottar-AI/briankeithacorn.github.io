Why did Jesus come at Christmas?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 9 moments from 7 messages. Here is what they say.

  • God planned it all along. Christmas didn’t start at the manger. God had been keeping his promise for thousands of years. Jesus was never plan B. 2 3
  • Jesus came to fix our biggest problem. We can’t earn our way back to God. So God sent his Son, fully God and fully human, to die for our sins and bring us back. 1 4
  • It’s a love story. God’s love is at the center of Christmas. Since he loved us, we are called to love others. 5
  • Don’t miss Jesus. Holiday happiness fades fast. The shepherds left everything to find Jesus. We can share him with others, even in small ways. 6 7 8 9
