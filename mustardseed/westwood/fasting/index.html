What does the Bible say about fasting?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors have not preached a whole sermon on fasting yet. But they talk about it in 10 moments from 8 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Fasting goes with prayer. Nehemiah fasted and prayed for months before God opened the door for him to act. 1 2 Anna worshiped God “with fasting and prayer night and day” while she waited for Jesus. She was seeking God with all her heart. 3
  • Jesus fasted. Before Jesus started his work, he went into the wilderness and fasted for 40 days. He was weak and hungry, and that is when Satan tempted him. 4 5 6
  • Fasting is not for showing off. In Jesus’ story, a Pharisee bragged, “I fast twice a week.” Fasting should have drawn him close to God. Instead he used it to admire himself. 7 8 9
  • God wants our whole heart. In Joel, God says, “Return to me with all your heart, with fasting.” God is patient and kind, and he welcomes us back. 10

These are the passages our pastors read or talk about in the clips. We also added Jesus’ own teaching on fasting from Matthew 6.

Nehemiah 1:4 · Nehemiah fasts and prays for his city (clips 1)

4As soon as I heard these words I sat down and wept and mourned for days, and I continued fasting and praying before the God of heaven.

Luke 2:36-38 · Anna worships with fasting and prayer (clips 3)

36And there was a prophetess, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was advanced in years, having lived with her husband seven years from when she was a virgin, 37and then as a widow until she was eighty-four. She did not depart from the temple, worshiping with fasting and prayer night and day. 38And coming up at that very hour she began to give thanks to God and to speak of him to all who were waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem.

Matthew 4:1-2 · Jesus fasts 40 days in the wilderness (clips 4, 5, 6)

1Then Jesus was led up by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. 2And after fasting forty days and forty nights, he was hungry.

Luke 18:9-14 · The Pharisee who bragged about fasting (clips 7, 8, 9)

9He also told this parable to some who trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and treated others with contempt: 10“Two men went up into the temple to pray, one a Pharisee and the other a tax collector. 11The Pharisee, standing by himself, prayed thus: ‘God, I thank you that I am not like other men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even like this tax collector. 12I fast twice a week; I give tithes of all that I get.’ 13But the tax collector, standing far off, would not even lift up his eyes to heaven, but beat his breast, saying, ‘God, be merciful to me, a sinner!’ 14I tell you, this man went down to his house justified, rather than the other. For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but the one who humbles himself will be exalted.”

Joel 2:12-13 · Return to God with fasting (clips 10)

12“Yet even now,” declares the LORD, “return to me with all your heart, with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning; 13and rend your hearts and not your garments.” Return to the LORD your God, for he is gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love; and he relents over disaster.
