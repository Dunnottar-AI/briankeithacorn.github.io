What do I do when I’m afraid?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 10 moments from 9 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Being afraid is normal. Even Nehemiah said, “I was very much afraid.” Fear is everywhere, from a hard day at work to scary news from a doctor. 7 10
  • Bring your fear to God. Prayer doesn’t just let go of fear. It puts our faith back in the right place. The battle belongs to God, not us. 1
  • Remember how big God is. Nehemiah told the people, “Do not be afraid. Remember the Lord.” If Jesus beat death, he can beat whatever we fear. When we only look at our fears, they start to define us. 2 5 6
  • Don’t let fear make your choices. We can take fearful thoughts captive and choose faith over fear. Jesus promised, “I am with you,” so we can live for him with courage. 3 4 8 9
