What is an idol, and could I have one?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 10 moments from 8 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Idols are not just statues. Today our idols can be jobs, money, comfort, phones, or popularity. We look to them for what only God can give. 1 2 6
  • Idols take God’s place. Israel tried to worship “God plus” other things. They used God’s good gifts to worship the gift instead of the Giver. 3 4 5
  • Turn from idols to God. The early church “turned to God from idols.” Holding on to idols makes us miss God’s grace. Following Jesus means chasing what he wants. 8 9 10
  • Giving helps. Being generous guards our hearts. The question is not what you own, but what owns you. 7

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.

Read it in the Bible

These are the passages our pastors read or talk about in the clips.

Isaiah 44:16-17 · Half the tree for a fire, half for an idol (clip 3)

16Half of it he burns in the fire. Over the half he eats meat; he roasts it and is satisfied. Also he warms himself and says, “Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire!” 17And the rest of it he makes into a god, his idol, and falls down to it and worships it. He prays to it and says, “Deliver me, for you are my god!”

Hosea 8:4 · Silver and gold made into idols (clip 4)

4They made kings, but not through me. They set up princes, but I knew it not. With their silver and gold they made idols for their own destruction.

Colossians 3:5 · Greed is idolatry (clip 6)

5Put to death therefore what is earthly in you: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry.

Luke 12:15 · Life is more than what you own (clip 6)

15And he said to them, “Take care, and be on your guard against all covetousness, for one’s life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions.”

1 Thessalonians 1:9-10 · Turning from idols to God (clip 8)

9For they themselves report concerning us the kind of reception we had among you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God, 10and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, Jesus who delivers us from the wrath to come.

Jonah 2:8-9 · Clinging to idols (clip 9)

8Those who pay regard to vain idols forsake their hope of steadfast love. 9But I with the voice of thanksgiving will sacrifice to you; what I have vowed I will pay. Salvation belongs to the LORD!
