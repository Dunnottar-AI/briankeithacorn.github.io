How can I have real peace?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 10 moments from 7 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Real peace comes from Jesus. Money and comfort can’t give lasting peace. Only Jesus, the Prince of Peace, can give the whole-life peace we want. 1 2 10
  • Jesus paid for our peace. “The punishment that brought us peace was on him.” Christmas reminds us that peace is a gift from Jesus himself. 4 8
  • Peace is more than quiet. God’s peace means well-being and flourishing. The world can give the look of peace without the real thing. 5 9
  • Give it to God and leave it there. Even in the darkest valley, we can be at peace because God is with us. Fear steals our peace, so we pray and let God carry it. 3 6 7

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
