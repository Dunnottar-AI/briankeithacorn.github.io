How do I fight temptation?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 9 moments from 8 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Temptation starts inside us. James says we are pulled away by our own wants. Small steps off the path can lead us far from God, so we need to own our choices and turn back early. 1 9
  • Everyone struggles. Even after we follow Jesus, temptation still comes, and sometimes we fall for the same thing again and again. But sin is not our boss anymore. 2 6 7
  • Stay close to Jesus. Growing is not about trying harder. It is about staying connected to Jesus and letting him change us. When we keep our eyes on him, sin starts to lose its shine. 3 5
  • Be ready, and don’t go alone. Satan tries to pull us off track, so we put on the armor of God. Walking with other believers who cheer us on makes the path easier. 4 8

Read it in the Bible

These are the passages our pastors read or talk about in the clips. We also added a passage or two that is central to this topic; those are marked.

James 1:13-15 · Where temptation comes from (clip 1)

13Let no one say when he is tempted, “I am being tempted by God,” for God cannot be tempted with evil, and he himself tempts no one. 14But each person is tempted when he is lured and enticed by his own desire. 15Then desire when it has conceived gives birth to sin, and sin when it is fully grown brings forth death.

Ephesians 6:10-11 · Put on the armor of God (clip 4)

10Finally, be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might. 11Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.

Matthew 4:1-2 · Jesus was tempted too (not read in these sermons)

1Then Jesus was led up by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. 2And after fasting forty days and forty nights, he was hungry.

1 Corinthians 10:13 · God gives a way out (not read in these sermons)

13No temptation has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond your ability, but with the temptation he will also provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.
