How can I get wisdom from God?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 9 moments from 7 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Wisdom comes from God. When you go after wisdom, you are going after God. Solomon knew he could not lead on his own, so he asked God for wisdom. 2 3
  • Jesus is wisdom. All the treasures of wisdom are found in Jesus. Wisdom is not just a set of rules. It is a Person. 1
  • Respect God first. Who we fear decides who we obey. “The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom.” God sees more than we do, and his ways are higher than ours. 4 5
  • Grow in it on purpose. We don’t drift into wisdom. We grow it by reading God’s Word, praying, and spending time with wise people. 6 7 8 9
