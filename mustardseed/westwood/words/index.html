How should I use my words?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 8 moments from 6 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Words are powerful. “Death and life are in the power of the tongue.” Our words can hurt, but they can also heal. 1 2
  • Gossip breaks things. Gossip and lies ruin trust and friendships. Followers of Jesus choose not to join in. 3 5 6 8
  • Speak life. What if we used our words to encourage people? When we get it wrong, saying “I’m sorry” is one of the strongest things we can do. 2 4
  • Kids carry our words. One day our kids will leave home carrying the words we said to them. Let them be words of life. 7
