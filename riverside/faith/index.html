What does it mean to have faith?

Faith means trusting God. Here is what our pastors have said about faith in our sermons. Tap a clip to hear it.

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 6 moments from 5 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Faith keeps trusting. Faith says, “Even when I lose things, even when I am not sure, I will still trust you, Lord.” 1
  • Faith steps out. Faith doesn’t wait until it’s safe. It moves because it trusts God’s heart, like Ruth did. 2 3
  • Little faith can grow. Jesus’ followers had little faith. After he rose from the dead, their faith became great. 4 5
  • Jesus sees our faith. When friends bring friends to Jesus, he sees their faith and is moved. 6

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
