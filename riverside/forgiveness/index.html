How do I forgive someone who hurt me?

Forgiving is hard. Here is what our pastors have said about forgiveness in our sermons. Tap a clip to hear it.

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 6 moments from 5 messages. Here is what they say.

  • God forgives first. Forgiveness is the greatest miracle Jesus does. No life is beyond God’s grace. 1 2
  • Jesus says forgive. When Jesus says forgive, we forgive, and we learn to love better. 3
  • Break the cycle. Hurting people back keeps everyone stuck. Pastor Mark shares how he chose to forgive his father. 4 5
  • Take a step today. Maybe God is asking you to forgive someone, or to ask someone to forgive you. 6

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
