How should I pray?

Prayer is talking with God. Here is what our pastors have said about prayer in our sermons. Tap a clip to hear it.

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 6 moments from 5 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Prayer changes us. Even when prayer does not change what happens, it changes us. So pray anyway. 1
  • Pray and act. We pray for our city and our neighbors. Then we ask: am I willing to be part of the answer? 2
  • Pray with others. God’s house is a house of prayer. If praying alone is hard, pray with a group. 3 5
  • Pray for your enemies. Jesus tells us to pray for people who hurt us. Jacob prayed when he was afraid, too. 4 6

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
