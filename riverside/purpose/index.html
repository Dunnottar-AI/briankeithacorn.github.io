What is God’s purpose for my life?

God made you on purpose, for a purpose. Here is what our pastors have said about it in our sermons. Tap a clip to hear it.

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 7 moments from 6 messages. Here is what they say.

  • No one else is you. No one else can do what God made you to do. 1
  • We all have a calling. Every believer is called to help find people who are lost, not just missionaries. 2 3 4
  • Point people to Jesus. John the Baptist’s purpose was to point people to Jesus. Ours is too. 5
  • One mistake doesn’t end it. Even after a bad choice, God’s grace gives your purpose back. God works in ordinary moments. 6 7

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
