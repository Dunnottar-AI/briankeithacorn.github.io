Why do I hurt, and does God care?

Life can be painful. Here is what our pastors have said about pain and suffering in our sermons. Tap a clip to hear it.

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 6 moments from 6 messages. Here is what they say.

  • God hears your pain. God feels it with you. He cares about our hurt and our seasons of loss. 1
  • Jesus understands. No one understood pain more than Jesus. He lived as a man and faced hard things too, so bring your pain to him. 2 4
  • Pain does not define you. Hard times can’t change who God says you are. When people hurt you, you look more like Jesus. 2 3
  • Notice others who hurt. The person smiling at church may be crying at home. Show them the kindness Jesus showed you. 5 6

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
