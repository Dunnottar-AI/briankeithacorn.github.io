Pray With Us

Pray along with Pastor Brett McNeill. Tap a prayer. The words light up as he prays, so you can pray with him, word by word.

“Our gracious and merciful God, we know that you…”

Before the sermon: Fellowship · Oct 12, 2026 · 0:48

Our gracious and merciful God, we know that you are great and greatly to be praised. We long to know you and your attributes and your character and your works. And it is these that that you have recorded for us in your word that you have preserved through the ages so that each generation might come Aresh and behold your grace, your love, and your power. And as we come to your word, we ask that you'd open our eyes and our hearts to behold its treasures. Allow us to gaze upon your beauty and splendor. humble us, encourage us, and strengthen us through Jesus Christ whom we meet in your word. We pray. Amen.

“Our great God of truth, our merciful savior, you…”

Before the sermon: Prayer · Oct 5, 2026 · 0:58

Our great God of truth, our merciful savior, you have told us to offer up our bodies as living and holy sacrifices, for such is holy and acceptable to you. It is our spiritual service of worship. We confess what you already know that we often struggle to do so. We are far too entangled in the affairs and the concerns of this world. We have our minds set on the things below rather than the things above. And so we struggle to follow you as we should. So we ask that you would renew our minds. Transform them through your word. Teach us to think as you think, to believe as you believe. Teach us to love what you love. Do this for us this morning as we listen to and as we meditate upon your word. We pray. Be with us now by your Holy Spirit. Amen.

“Father, we know that your word is good. It…”

Before the sermon: Sorrows Turned to Joy · Aug 10, 2026 · 1:07

Father, we know that your word is good. It is perfect. It gives life. It enlightens the eyes. It renews the soul. But we know that it can be and has been abused. We know that sinful man has used your glorious word for his own sinful ends. We want to hear your word as you want it to be heard. But that can also scare us because your word is about Jesus and his need to suffer before he entered into his glory. And if we're honest, we want Jesus without the cross. We want his kingdom without tribulation. We want comfort more than we want character. And so we ask that you would crucify these desires. May our only desire be for Jesus Christ and him crucified. May every word that comes from this pulpit reflect your word as you intended it. Guard my lips. Open our hearts and our ears that we might see Jesus. We pray. Amen.

“Our great God of truth, we confess that we…”

Before the sermon: The Cross in the Family · May 18, 2026 · 0:54

Our great God of truth, we confess that we are prone, we are disposed to believe lies and not the truth. We are easily swayed and led astray. We give ear to voices that we ought not. We believe things that are simply untrue. We believe things that you have clearly denied. And we foolishly believe that you are limited by our strength, constrained by our sin, and that somehow our wickedness is greater than your mercy. And so, as we turn to your word, we ask that you would root out all lies. You would destroy all impostors for the truth, and that you would renew our minds in the knowledge of your truth. “Heavenly Father, eternal God, you have told us that…”

Before the sermon: First-Fruits · Apr 20, 2026 · 0:41

Heavenly Father, eternal God, you have told us that all flesh is like the grass. It is a breath and then it is gone. But your word, what we behold in our hands, is eternal. It is something that has been around long before us and will be around long after us. It It forever. So, we ask that you would grant that we might give it our undivided attention. That we would be receptive to all that it says. That our beliefs, our understanding, all of our expectations would all be brought into accord with your word. We ask this all in the name of your son who is the word made flesh. Amen.

“God, your word is a lamp to our feet.…”

Before the sermon: The Risen Physician · Apr 6, 2026 · 0:46

God, your word is a lamp to our feet. It is bread for our souls. We live by every word that proceeds from your mouth. And we long that your word would richly dwell in our hearts, that it would be the meditation of our hearts day and night. And that we would teach it for generations to come. For we know that heaven and earth will pass away, but your word will not. And it does not return void, but accomplishes all that you purpose for it. And so, as we draw near to you in your word, we ask that you would open the eyes of our hearts, that you would give us understanding, that you would grant us faith, and that you would teach us to delight in all that delights you, we pray. Amen.
