Why trust the Bible?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“Now, the qualification is from preaching as it is derived from scripture. That's that's the that's the essential qualification. Preaching derived from scripture is the word of God. Preaching not derived from scripture is not the word of God. So I'm not saying that he has the infallible, you know, perfect ability to only proclaim truth. Every minister knows we make errors. But the focus of our work is to proclaim the text of scripture to you.”

Steve Baugh · Calm Seas of Truth · Ephesians 4:10-16 · Sep 28, 2026 · Watch this part (8:32) →

“He says it wasn't just Jesus that had foretold this. Paul says, "No, this was foretold by the Old Testament scriptures." To the Corinthians he said this, "I delivered you of first importance what I also received, that Christ died for our sins in accordance with the scriptures, that he was buried, and that he was raised on the third day in accordance with the scriptures." When when Paul says scriptures, he means the Old Testament scriptures. The law and the prophets. He's saying, "If we listen to the Old Testament scriptures written before Jesus ever came, we would understand not only that the Messiah must die and be raised on the third day, but we would also understand why." And so for the last several years we have been pausing each year to ask, "Where?”

Brett McNeill · The Risen Physician · Hosea 6:1-10; Matthew 9:1-13 · Apr 6, 2026 · Watch this part (6:28) →

“The command of Esther confirmed these practices of PUM and it was recorded in writing. So ends the reading of our God's word. Let us ask his blessing on our time in his word this morning. Father, your word is a lamp to our feet. We understand that man does not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from your mouth. And so we long for your word to dwell in our hearts richly. We long for it to be the meditation of our hearts day and night. That we would teach your word to generations to come. We know that heaven and earth will pass away but not your word.”
