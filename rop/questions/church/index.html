Why does the church matter?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“And so as the church grows, as people bow their knees to Christ, the temple, that is the church, is growing to fill the earth, the very thing Adam was supposed to do. This is why admitting people to and removing them from membership in the church is called the keys of the kingdom. Because the church is how God's kingdom is manifested in this world. When you are admitted to the church, you are admitted to the kingdom of God. It's not what people see when they look at the church. They don't see, "Wow, look at that amazing kingdom." The church is not impressive in the world's eyes.”

Brett McNeill · The Kingdom of the Second Adam · 1 Corinthians 15:20-34 · Apr 27, 2026 · Watch this part (18:08) →

“That's the first lie that separates people from the church. The second is found in verses 21- 24. It's thinking that you don't need the church. The first lie is that the church doesn't need you. The second lie is that you don't need the church. And you know where this starts, don't you? You've been hurt. You've been burned. You've been let down. You tell everyone to church, I've certainly experienced hurt. I don't want to say your pain isn't valid. It is valid. But does it mean that you don't need the church? Are are you so strong, so self-sufficient that you truly don't need others?”

Brett McNeill · Fellowship · 1 Corinthians 12:12-27, Matthew 22:34-40 · Oct 12, 2026 · Watch this part (17:50) →

“It's easy to forget that most of the church's history they have suffered great persecution. That's still true in much of the world today. We pray every week for the persecuted church because our brothers and sisters are enduring heavy persecution. The persecuted church has long held to this song for comfort in the midst of it. Here's what Augustine says about Psalm 124. God is blessed when the church thanks him for the gift to the church of martyrs who were victorious over their enemies. I'll read that again because we don't talk like this.”

Brett McNeill · Humility · Psalm 124 · Jul 20, 2026 · Watch this part (27:30) →

“And as they're together each and every week doing these things together as the church of Jesus Christ, many are gathered into the church. And then again in chapter 3 verse 1, the events that lead to the prayer meeting that we're looking at this morning. It says Peter and John were going up to the temple at the hour of prayer. And then God through them heals someone, they proclaim the gospel, and many are saved. We see this pattern again and again in Acts, but we see this pattern again and again throughout the history of the church. When the church prays, the power of God is unleashed in the world.”

John Shaw · Kingdom Prayer · Acts 4:23-31 · Jun 22, 2026 · Watch this part (5:44) →
