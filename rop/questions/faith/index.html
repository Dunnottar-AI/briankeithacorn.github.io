What does it mean to have faith?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“Those who have a credible profession of faith. Only those who have confessed faith in Jesus Christ and by their lives demonstrated that faith to be real are allowed to come. Those for whom that is not yet true must wait until it is. This is how the Lord's supper differs from baptism. And the question is why? Well, there are many benefits. Let me let me give you three. The first is that it keeps before our children their need to put their faith and their trust in Jesus Christ. That's not mean.”

Brett McNeill · Breaking Bread with Sinners · Romans 4:1-12 · Sep 14, 2026 · Watch this part (27:44) →

“That's why he gives us that invitation to come. And he says, "Come to me in your weariness, in the burdens that you bear." And he says, "I will give you rest." The language we often use in the Lord's supper is nourishment for our faith, strengthening for our faith. He will give us rest. He takes the bread and he breaks it and he gives to his disciples saying, "Take and eat this my body broken for you. Do this in remembrance of me. If you are a member of Christ Church, whether it be here at Reformation Church or another Christ preaching church, if you've been baptized in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, if indeed you've professed your faith in Christ, even if you know your own weakness in walking”

Mark Sumpter · Guest sermon · Sep 22, 2025 · Watch this part (43:48) →

“Why do we pray for people with chronic health issues every Sunday at church? Because it can be hard to keep your focus on eternal things when you are so laid down with temporal suffering. And the most important thing your friends can pray for is that your faith would not just endure but grow in the midst of your hardship because suffering can grow your faith as well. And Paul serves as a wonderful example of this. he suffered some from some ailment of the eyes. We're not sure exactly what it was.”

Brett McNeill · Prayer · Philippians 4:4-8, James 5:13-18 · Oct 5, 2026 · Watch this part (26:10) →

“But what should I do?" Should pray. Martin Lloyd-Jones said quite regularly something like this, "The ultimate test of our profession of faith is our prayer life." When we face struggles and trials and tribulations and opposition from the world in our Christian faith and our Christian testimony, we should lead with prayer. But we sing this, don't we? What a friend we have in Jesus. Have we trials and temptations? Is there trouble anywhere?”

John Shaw · Kingdom Prayer · Acts 4:23-31 · Jun 22, 2026 · Watch this part (11:02) →
