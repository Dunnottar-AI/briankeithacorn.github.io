What do I do when I feel afraid?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“Fear of the Lord is when the thing that scares you more than anything else is the idea of being without him. If you fear losing anything more than the Lord, you will live for that thing, you it will control your heart and your mind and your life. That's the power of fear. This is why verse one says, "Blessed is everyone who fears the Lord, who walks in his ways." Because true fear of the Lord leads us to walk in his ways. It's not saying that walking in the Lord's ways earns our blessing. It's saying that those who fear the Lord will honor him. This is how we know someone truly fears the Lord.”

Brett McNeill · Blessed Fear · Psalm 128 · Sep 7, 2026 · Watch this part (29:32) →

“Fears are powerful. If we don't fear God, we will fear something else. And that fear will rule us. It's in worship that we enter into God's presence. Something that's very uncomfortable for sinners. But God doesn't leave us in our sin. Neither does he pretend it's not there. He confronts it so that we might confess it and seek his grace. When we do that, we find the very grace we're looking for. But it takes brutal honesty to get there. So last time two weeks ago we looked at the basic structure of the worship service and how God has crafted it to remind us of these most important truths that every week our sin is confronted and forgiven.”

Brett McNeill · Sunday's Shadow · Luke 11:1-3 · Feb 23, 2026 · Watch this part (11:31) →

“But even then, she fears she's asking too much. Really, what she fears is that she's not worth what it will cost her king. Am I worth hundreds of millions of dollars? That's what she's wrestling with. Perhaps your prayers have sounded like that. worried that God doesn't remember your name. Hi, God. It's me, Brett. You remember? Maybe you're worried you're asking too much, that it's too costly, that you just shouldn't bother him.”

Brett McNeill · The Fall of Haman · Esther 5:9-14; 6:14-7:10 · Nov 10, 2025 · Watch this part (22:56) →

“They needed to come out and be holy. They needed to be different from the nations around them. They needed to stop taking their cues from the sinful world. And they needed to think about eternity. They needed to learn to fear the right things, to place their fears in the right places. That's reverence. And that's why they needed to rebuild the temple because it's in worship where reverence is learned. It's in worship where they learn what lasts and what doesn't last. What matters and what doesn't matter. what is worthy of our fears and what isn't. So, it's no surprise that our passage in in Hebrews 12 quotes Haggi in verse 27 and immediately after that says, "So, let us therefore offer acceptable worship with reverence and awe.”

Brett McNeill · Reverent Worship · Hebrews 12:25-29 · Feb 2, 2026 · Watch this part (9:23) →
