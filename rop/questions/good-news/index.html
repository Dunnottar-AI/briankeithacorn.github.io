What is the good news?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“It's rooted in the truth of the gospel. It's saturated with the content of the gospel. It's focused on the advance of the gospel. It communicates the truth of the gospel as they trust, as they lean on Christ. And it prays for that gospel to go out into the world so that many might be saved. It's gospel-driven. It's also kingdom-centered. You can imagine many other things that they might have prayed. I don't think that as we read this, we understand this to be the full content of their prayer, but you notice as the prayer is summarized for us, they don't pray for safety.”

John Shaw · Kingdom Prayer · Acts 4:23-31 · Jun 22, 2026 · Watch this part (12:27) →

“And that's where we have the problem. People may not mean to make the fall the point on which everything else depends. And necessary for what takes place in the ultimately for Christ to come. But that is what happens. If you can't say that the good news actually comes before the bad news. Then the good news is made dependent upon the bad. The reality you are taught in scripture is that the gospel does not begin with the fall. That's not where scripture begins. Where does scripture begin? It begins with God.”

Andrew Davis · A World of Love · Genesis 1:1, 1 John 4:7-17 · Jul 27, 2026 · Watch this part (36:03) →

“God has made a a through Jesus Christ a covenant of grace where we relate to God not by how good we are, how well we obey, but how good Jesus is and how well he has obeyed. And that's what we call the gospel. good news. But before we can get to the good news, we have to deal with the bad news. We've talked about it many times and we've talked about it in the book of Esther. Our greatest problem in life is not our physical enemies. It's not our political enemies. It's not political parties. Our greatest problem is not physical sickness and disease.”

Brett McNeill · The New Covenant · Esther 8:3-10 · Nov 17, 2025 · Watch this part (8:38) →

“They were not ignorant and they knew what they were doing was wrong. The Jews were saying things like, "We have the scriptures." And Paul says, "Well, yes, you had them. Were you listening to them and following them?" They were saying things like, "We have circumcision. We are children of Abraham." And here in chapter 4, he's asking them if they really understood what circumcision was about. So the first eight verses are given over to reminding them of the gospel that Abraham believed, the gospel message.”

Brett McNeill · Breaking Bread with Sinners · Romans 4:1-12 · Sep 14, 2026 · Watch this part (12:06) →
