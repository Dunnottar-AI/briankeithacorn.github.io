What is grace?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“Not as a demand with no presumption that God will give you exactly what you want. That's not the point. Hebrews says it this way. Let us then with confidence draw near to the throne of grace that we might receive mercy and find grace to help in our time of need. Now details matter. What do we hope to find when we draw near to God in prayer? Mercy and grace. Not what we deserve, not what we are entitled to, but God's good gifts. And here's the thing about gifts.”

Brett McNeill · Prayer · Philippians 4:4-8, James 5:13-18 · Oct 5, 2026 · Watch this part (14:08) →

“None of what he's calling them to is possible unless God gives them the strength. He is enabling them to walk as his children in this life. In other words, there is no bootstrap, pull yourself up by your bootstrap theology in Paul. They need Jesus' strength and his grace. That must be, their daily prayer. They must seek his strength to do all these things, cuz it's hard. It's easy to be angry, angry, selfish, and bitter. It is hard to be sacrificial, gracious, and kind. Grace be with you. You need God's grace.”

Brett McNeill · Remember My Chains · Colossians 4:2-18 · Jun 1, 2026 · Watch this part (31:24) →

“And most importantly, I'll try to reiterate this often, but everything we hear today should be driven by a call to God's grace. Everything we hear should drive us to Jesus for his grace and his strength. It is so easy to hear instructions like this and think God is just waiting for us to get our acts together. Everything Paul says to parents and to children is in the context of his call to compassion and kindness and humility and meekness and patience and love and forgiveness. In other words, without the grace of Christ, we have no hope. It's true for children and it's true for parents.”

Brett McNeill · The Cross in the Family · Colossians 3:20-21 · May 18, 2026 · Watch this part (5:39) →

“It this new covenant is about grace, not justice. Second, it will have the power to change hearts. This new covenant will have the power to transform us and make us like what we long to be. telling people what to do isn't enough. We need transforming grace if we are ever to change. And that's what the new covenant promises. This is God's answer to the problem of sin. A different way of relating forgiveness instead of works, grace instead of law. And the question, the only question that matters is which covenant are you under?”

Brett McNeill · The New Covenant · Esther 8:3-10 · Nov 17, 2025 · Watch this part (16:30) →
