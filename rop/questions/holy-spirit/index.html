Who is the Holy Spirit?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“Therefore, be alert. So notice, notice that same thing in Acts 20 that we see in Ephesians 4. The Holy Spirit has made you overseers of the church. You have an appointment by God confirmed through the Holy Spirit. So you have this calling for the church of God. This is not, you know, volunteer thing where the pastor just is he just happens to stand up one day. Oh, I'll do it. You know, I'll be your pastor. And then but he doesn't have a divine call. This is why in our presetary when we examine men as we did a couple of days ago we're trying to discern a calling from God to do this and then you know evidencing it and that they will teach faithfully from scripture.”

Steve Baugh · Calm Seas of Truth · Ephesians 4:10-16 · Sep 28, 2026 · Watch this part (13:51) →

“By the grace of God at work in us, we believe that God does what he says he's going to do. And isn't that what he does in this passage? They they pray these things and as we get to verse 31, it says, "And when they had prayed." And I think we should understand it in this way. And immediately after they prayed, the place in which they were gathered together was shaken, clear and confirming signs of God's presence, and they were filled with the Holy Spirit and continued to speak the word of God with boldness. So, we pray with confidence, and we pray these kinds of prayers, not only there are other things we're called to pray for, but we're called to pray gospel-centered, kingdom-centered prayers with a desire that God gives us by the power of his Holy Spirit to see Jesus Christ honored and glorified as more and more people believe and trust in him.”

John Shaw · Kingdom Prayer · Acts 4:23-31 · Jun 22, 2026 · Watch this part (30:26) →
