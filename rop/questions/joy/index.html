How can I have joy?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“We sang up from the grave he arose. We have joy. We have great joy. But greater joy is to follow. The the joy of verse three is just a foretaste of what is to come. And so it is with us. There is much joy in following Jesus. His message of grace brings us great joy. But all of this is a shadow of the abundance that we will experience in heaven. But the road to glory is the road of the cross of hardship.”

“It was time to head to Jerusalem to gather at the temple. And the psalmist explodes with joy. Like he's been just waiting for the for the feast to come and the journey to start. And and the can't and the and the call went, "Let's head out." And he's so excited. He gets to be with his God. He gets to enter in. “Serve the Lord. Look at 12. Rejoice in hope. Where do we rejoice? We rejoice in our God. Be patient in tribulation. Our God is providentially leading us. He's Lord. We're called to patience in times of trial. And then lordship is also characterized. Give yourselves to be constant in prayer. Show a dependency upon the Lord. Lord, you're our God. And you see what Paul is doing. It's like when he layers in the recipe of different ingredients that characterize the life of the church and the life of the Christian.”

“They would have joyful feasts there. Deuteronomy 12:12. They would they would they would exalt in his kindness and his mercy. How could they not? The God of all creation has called them into his presence to know him in his salvation and his love. How how could they be anything but joyful? And so these twin realities, reverent fear and joy, define what it means for God's people to enter into his presence and to stand on holy ground. And and we struggle with that. like our tendency is to pick one and exclude the other.
