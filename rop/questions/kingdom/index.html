What is God's kingdom?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“I'm sure you are familiar with that phrase in the Bible and that it is a big theme in scripture. You might remember that John the Baptist came saying, "the time is fulfilled and the kingdom of God is at hand. Repent and believe the gospel." You might remember that the message of the apostles was summarized as preaching the kingdom. Or that Jesus said that if he cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon us. And then he cast out demons, and you can do the math. You might remember as well that Jesus said his kingdom is not of this world and will not come with obvious visible signs that can be observed.”

Brett McNeill · The Kingdom of the Second Adam · 1 Corinthians 15:20-34 · Apr 27, 2026 · Watch this part (9:24) →

“Take it to the Lord in prayer. And as they lift their voices in prayer, God unleashes the power of the kingdom through his church. So, what kind of prayer do they offer up to God in these verses? That's what I want us to consider more specifically this morning, the quality, the character of the prayer that they offer to God. I think we could describe it, sum it up in this way. It's gospel-driven prayer. It's kingdom-centered prayer. So, what do I mean by that? Gospel-driven prayer.”

John Shaw · Kingdom Prayer · Acts 4:23-31 · Jun 22, 2026 · Watch this part (11:45) →

“How often do we come into prayer wanting to build our kingdom, our agenda, our vision for the future. You can't honestly pray your kingdom come without surrender in your heart because what is God's kingdom? It's it's the new creation. To pray for God's kingdom to come is to long for the return of Christ where he vanquishes evil and we live in his blessed presence for all eternity. More than that, it's a request that God's church would be built because that's where that eternal kingdom, the new creation is manifested in this world.”

Brett McNeill · Sunday's Shadow · Luke 11:1-3 · Feb 23, 2026 · Watch this part (15:17) →

“Let me know what do you want. whatever it is up to half my kingdom. That's incredible. he's not offering to divide his kingdom in two. He's not saying if you want me to, I will cut the north off from the south. You can take the south. I'll take the north. We'll never talk again. He's offering to share his kingdom with her in fullness. So this what is his is hers. He's making her equal co-air in his kingdom. And it's the opposite of what she expected. It's the opposite of what she feared.”

Brett McNeill · Drawing near · Esther 5:1-8, 6:1-13 · Nov 3, 2025 · Watch this part (9:03) →
