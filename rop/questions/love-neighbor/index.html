How do I love other people?

Here is what was said about this in our sermons. These are the preachers' own words. Tap a clip to hear it.

“first and the second great commandment that if we want to love God, we must love our neighbor. If we want to serve God, we must serve our neighbor. Christian obedience isn't private and hidden and something you can do alone. It's communal. It's done in community. So, what does it look like? Now, obviously, the Ten Commandments teach us what it looks like, but there's another rich resource in scripture to tell us what loving our neighbor looks like. And it's all the one another passages in scripture.”

Brett McNeill · Fellowship · 1 Corinthians 12:12-27, Matthew 22:34-40 · Oct 12, 2026 · Watch this part (9:57) →

“That the time would come when they would grow bored with what God had commanded and they would seek out something new and different and exciting. and they would look at how other people worshiped their gods and be tempted to adopt the same practices. Not necessarily worshiping their neighbors gods, but worshiping the God of Israel with practices adopted from their neighbors. God warned them that this would happen and he told them not to give in. They were to do all that he had commanded and only what he had commanded, neither adding to his commands nor taking away.”

Brett McNeill · Acceptable Worship · Hebrews 12:25-29 · Jan 19, 2026 · Watch this part (9:49) →
