How do I handle anger?

← All questions

In short

  • “Yeah, absolutely." But instead, God says, "My anger will turn that when they return to me, my anger will go away and compassion will come in.” 1
  • “For they have provoked you to anger in the presence of the builders.” 3
  • “… Do we respond in kindness and patience or in control and anger do we respond in fear or in faith maybe you know we just …” 5
  • “Maybe you're driving and you're quick to anger and judgement when you see someone else driving poorly.” 8
  • “Is it a default to anger, pride, or another critical emotion?” 9
