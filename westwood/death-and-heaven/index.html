What happens when we die? Is heaven real?

← All questions

In short

  • “That every time that we see a grave, we go to a funeral, we drive past a funeral home, is that we are reminded that this world is not the way that it was supposed to be.” 1
  • “That we serve a God who walked out of the grave and so therefore we don't have to walk around with our heads hanging low believing that our savior died for us.” 2
  • “And on the third day, Jesus walked out of this tomb, making it empty because death could not hold it.” 3
  • “This is why in 1 Corinthians chapter 15, as Paul is talking and celebrating the resurrection, he makes these verses kind of a battle cry.” 4
  • “And that is our testimony in Jesus Christ that through Jesus' death, we will experience the fullness of life through the glory of heaven.” 9
