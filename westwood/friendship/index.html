What does the Bible say about friends?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 8 moments from 5 messages. Here is what they say.

  • Friends have power. The people we spend time with will either build us up and lead us toward wisdom, or pull us away from God. 1
  • Jesus is the best friend. Jesus is a friend you’ll never regret. Abraham was even called “a friend of God.” 2 3
  • Real friendship takes work. It means being honest, even when that feels scary. When a friendship starts to drift, lean in and talk about it. 5 6 7
  • Don’t pull away. Sin pushes us to hide and be alone. God gives us chances to open our lives to others. Parents can help kids grow good friendships. 4 8

This summary is in our own words. The numbers link to the clips, so you can hear each pastor say it.
