Westwood on your phone

Add Westwood to your phone

It takes less than a minute. Then Westwood opens from your home screen, just like an app.

You're all set. Westwood is on your phone. Open it from your home screen any time.

Please open this page in Safari. On iPhone, only Safari can add Westwood to your home screen. Copy this link and paste it into Safari:
briankeithacorn.github.io/westwood/install/

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share. It is the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll down.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu. It is the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
