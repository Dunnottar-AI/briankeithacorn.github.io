Should Christians judge other people?

← All questions

In short

Our pastors talk about this in 9 moments from 3 messages. Here is what they say.

  • There is a difference. Knowing right from wrong is good. Being judgmental means having a critical attitude toward people. Jesus warns us about that. 1 2
  • Look at yourself first. Jesus said to take the log out of your own eye first. Humility keeps us from being hypocrites. 3 4 5
  • Correct with compassion. Sometimes we do need to speak up and help someone. But correcting without compassion crushes people. Love must be real, not an act. 6 7 8
  • Jesus loved the judged. The woman at the well had felt the stares and heard the whispers. Jesus met her with truth and love. 9

1“Judge not, that you be not judged. 2For with the judgment you pronounce you will be judged, and with the measure you use it will be measured to you. 3Why do you see the speck that is in your brother’s eye, but do not notice the log that is in your own eye? 4Or how can you say to your brother, ‘Let me take the speck out of your eye,’ when there is the log in your own eye? 5You hypocrite, first take the log out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to take the speck out of your brother’s eye.
