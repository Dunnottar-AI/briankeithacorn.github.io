What does the Bible say about leadership?

← All questions

In short

  • “Jesus, the son of God, who came to this earth to wash feet, to eat with sinners, to talk with children, all things that wasn't done at that time that no leader would even have to dare to have time.” 2
  • “We either will lead people to get closer with God or we'll lead people away from God.” 3
  • “I mean if we want to be godly leaders in our companies in the marketplace, what the best thing we can do is to find other godly wise leaders and do life with them.” 4
  • “See, the truth is wholehearted trust leads to renewed strength.” 5
  • “If he defeats sin, then he has the power to change your heart and your desires and you lead your life into a new direction.” 7
