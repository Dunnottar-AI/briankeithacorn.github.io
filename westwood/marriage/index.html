What our pastors have said about marriage

← All questions

In short

  • Marriage is a daily choice. Westwood's pastors describe marriage as something built day by day: “a daily choosing each other,” “a daily loving each other,” and “daily resolving conflict.” 1
  • It is a covenant, not a symbol. A wedding ring is a sign, but “our marriage is about a relationship.” In Hosea, God uses a marriage as “a mirror” of his own personal, pursuing love for his people. 23
  • Grace and apology matter. In a counseling story, the breakthrough came when a wife finally said, “But you've never apologized.” Return and repentance open the way forward. 4
  • Don't do it alone. “If we want to be faithful husbands and wives… we find other faithful husbands and wives and we do life with them.” Marriages built on God's word are also a target for division, so guard them. 56
  • It starts with worship. “You want to woo your wife? Worship your God.” Honoring God shapes who we date, who we marry, and the sacred union of marriage itself. 789
