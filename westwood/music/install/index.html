Add Westwood Worship to your phone

It takes less than a minute. Then an app called Worship shows up on your home screen. Tap it to open Westwood Worship. It is separate from Westwood Sermons, so you can have both.

You're all set. Worship is on your home screen.

Please open this page in Safari. On iPhone, only Safari can add it to your home screen. Copy this link into Safari:
briankeithacorn.github.io/westwood/music/install/

iPhone

  1. Open briankeithacorn.github.io/westwood/music/ in Safari.
  2. Tap Share. It is the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll down.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open briankeithacorn.github.io/westwood/music/ in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu. It is the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
