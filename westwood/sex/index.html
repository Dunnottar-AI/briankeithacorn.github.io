What does the Bible say about sex?

← All questions

In short

  • “So Paul is saying, listen, use sex as it was created to be, and it will bring warmth.” 1
  • “If you want to engage in sexual immorality, go for it.” 2
  • “They lived in this culture of immorality and this moral chaos where nothing was sacred.” 3
  • “Therefore, God gave them up in the lust of their hearts to impurity to the dishonoring of their bodies among themselves.” 4
  • “Purity removes the cloudiness of sin so we can perceive God's presence, his character, and his guidance more fully.” 5

These are our pastors’ own words. Tap a number to hear that part.
